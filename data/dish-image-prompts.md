# Dish image prompts — Nano Banana 2 (Google AI Studio)

**Goal:** one photorealistic image per dish (60), faithful to the recipe shown in the app.
**Language:** English (Nano Banana 2 follows natural-language photographic prose best).

## Nano Banana 2 tips
- **Style: épuré / food-hero** — the plated dish fills the frame on a plain cream surface, no props, no
  cutlery, no glassware, no napkins. Clean and consistent for the app (baked into every prompt).
- These are written as flowing scene descriptions (not keyword salad) — that's what NB2 handles best.
- **Generate `p-steak-frites` first**, then attach that image as a **style reference** and ask NB2 to
  "match the lighting, angle and style of the reference" for the other 59 → keeps the whole set cohesive.
- Set **aspect ratio 1:1** in AI Studio (or keep the "square 1:1" already in each prompt).
- If a dish looks off, add ONE more concrete detail (a color, a texture, a garnish) — don't rewrite the whole thing.
- Export each square image and name it with the dish `id` → **`public/dishes/<id>.png`**
  (e.g. `public/dishes/p-steak-frites.png`). Once the folder is filled, I wire them into the app.

## Style clause (already appended to every prompt below)
`Professional food photography, the entire dish fully visible and centered, with an even margin of plain background around it and nothing cropped or cut off at the edges, the food as the only subject on a plain warm cream-toned surface with a clean minimal softly-blurred background, no extra props, no cutlery, no drink glasses, no napkins. Shot on a 50mm lens at f/2.8, soft diffused natural daylight, three-quarter 45-degree angle, rich appetizing natural colors, shallow depth of field, square 1:1 composition, no text, no hands, no logo.`
